Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Short version: Bookmark Tab Manager collects absolutely no personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no tracking of any kind.

1. Information We Collect

Bookmark Tab Manager collects no personal information whatsoever. We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact information
  • Your bookmarks or browsing history
  • Your location or device information
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Cookies or tracking data

2. How Your Data Is Stored

All data created in Bookmark Tab Manager — including your bookmarks, folders, spaces, settings and preferences — is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is never accessible to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the extension

3. Third-Party Services

Bookmark Tab Manager uses the following third-party services for specific features:

  • Open-Meteo API (weather.open-meteo.com) — Used to fetch weather data. Your approximate GPS coordinates are sent to this service only when the weather widget is enabled. Open-Meteo is a free, open-source weather API with no data retention policy. See their privacy policy at open-meteo.com.
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — Used to convert GPS coordinates to a city name for weather display. No personal data is stored by this service.
  • Google Favicon Service — Used to display website icons next to bookmarks. The domain names of your bookmarked websites are sent to Google's favicon service. No personal data is included.
  • Google Fonts — Used to load custom fonts if selected. Font requests go to Google's servers per their standard privacy policy.
  • Lemon Squeezy — Used for Pro license key validation. When you enter a license key, it is validated against Lemon Squeezy's API. Only the license key itself is transmitted — no personal information.

4. Permissions Used

Bookmark Tab Manager requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — To save your bookmarks and settings locally in your browser
  • geolocation — Only used for the weather widget, only when enabled by you

We do not request access to your browsing history, tabs, or any other sensitive permissions.

5. Pro License

If you purchase Bookmark Tab Manager Pro, your purchase is processed by Lemon Squeezy. We receive only your license key status — not your payment information, name, or email address. Please refer to Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy for information about how they handle your purchase data.

6. Children's Privacy

Bookmark Tab Manager does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since we collect no data at all, this is inherently satisfied.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Bookmark Tab Manager after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
