FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Bookmark Tab Manager really free?
Yes — the free plan is free forever with no time limit. You get unlimited spaces, folders, bookmarks, import/export, live clock, weather, dark/light themes, tags, privacy mode, broken link checker and more. No credit card required.
What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free gives you all the core bookmark management features. Pro unlocks visual customization (50 gradient themes, video backgrounds, Unsplash photos, particles), productivity tools (AI categorize, heatmap, streak, weekly report), child lock with PIN, ambient sounds, dual timezone clock, unlimited incognito, export as Markdown and more.
How many devices can I use Pro on?
Monthly and Yearly plans allow activation on up to 5 devices. Lifetime plan allows unlimited activations (999 devices). Each device uses one activation slot when you enter your license key.
How do I get my Pro license after payment?
Immediately after payment via Razorpay, our system automatically generates a unique license key (BTM-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX) and emails it to the address you entered. Open a new tab → click the 🔒 Free badge → enter your key → Activate.
Can I try Pro before buying?
Yes! Click the 🔒 Free badge on any new tab → "Try Pro Free for 1 Hour". All Pro features unlock immediately for 60 minutes with no payment required.
Is my data private? Where is it stored?
All your bookmarks, settings and preferences are stored locally in Chrome's storage on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. Your data never leaves your browser. Only license key validation communicates with our server (Supabase).
Does it work in incognito mode?
Chrome does not allow extensions to override the new tab page in incognito mode — this is a Chrome security policy that applies to all new tab extensions, not specific to BTM. However, you can still open any bookmark directly in an incognito window using the right-click menu → "Open in Incognito". This works without any extra settings.
Will it replace my existing bookmarks?
No. BTM works alongside Chrome's native bookmarks. You can import your Chrome bookmarks into BTM with one click, but your original Chrome bookmarks remain untouched. You can also export BTM bookmarks back to Chrome HTML format anytime.
What payment methods are supported?
We use Razorpay — India's leading payment gateway. Accepted: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), all Debit/Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), Net Banking and more. Payments are in INR (₹).
Can I get a refund?
We offer a 1-hour free trial so you can test all Pro features before buying. Due to the digital nature of license keys, we generally do not offer refunds after activation. If you have an issue, reach out via our Telegram community — we'll make it right.
What is the Unsplash background feature?
Pro users can enable auto-changing Unsplash photos as their background — choose a category (Nature, City, Space, Mountains, Ocean, Abstract, Dark, Minimal) and BTM loads a fresh high-quality photo every new tab or once daily. Requires an Unsplash API key (free).
How does AI Auto-Categorize work?
When you right-click a bookmark and choose "AI Categorize", BTM sends the bookmark title and URL to Claude AI (by Anthropic) which suggests the most appropriate folder from your existing folders. It's fast, accurate and saves time when organizing large collections.
